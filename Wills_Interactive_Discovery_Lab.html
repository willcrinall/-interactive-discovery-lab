Experiment 01

🍋 Lemon & spinach pasta

Choose your number of diners:

Method

  1. Boil salted water and cook the pasta until al dente. Reserve a mug of pasta water.
  2. Warm olive oil in a pan, briefly soften minced garlic, then wilt the spinach.
  3. Toss in the pasta, lemon zest, lemon juice, Parmesan and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Season, taste, and serve immediately.

Tip: The checkboxes are interactive; change the number of diners to reset the list.

Experiment 03

⚽ Offside, made visible

Drag the blue attacker left or right on the pitch. The pass is made at this instant. The yellow players are defenders.

Simplified example: the attacker is in the opposition half, the ball is with the passer, and the yellow defender at x=260 is the second-last opponent. Being in an offside position is not itself an offence; involvement in active play matters.
